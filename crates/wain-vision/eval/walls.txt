# Walls in public photos, marked up by hand, for scoring `find_obstructions`.
# See `examples/evaluate.rs`, which reads this file.
#
#   photo <id> <file name on Wikimedia Commons>
#   wall <width mm> <height mm> <top-left> <top-right> <bottom-right> <bottom-left>
#   <kind> <from left mm> <above floor mm> <width mm> <height mm>
#
# Corners are fractions of the photo's width and height, as x,y. A photo can
# have more than one wall; each takes the lines that follow it. Kinds are
# door, opening, window, outlet and switch, measured to the outside of the
# casing or cover plate. `other` marks something that is neither expected nor
# a mistake to find: furniture against the wall, a picture, a fireplace.
#
# The photos themselves are not kept here. `scripts/fetch-eval-photos.sh`
# downloads each one from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:<file name>,
# where its author and licence are given.
#
# None of these rooms was measured. Where the ceiling is out of shot the
# "wall" is the part in view, and sizes were worked out from things of known
# size in the photo: cover plates, door leaves. Positions are good to about
# 40 mm.
#
# The first eighteen photos were used to tune the detectors. The last three
# (d096, c109, d154) and the second walls of d048 and d050 were marked up
# afterwards as a check, though d096 has since informed a change too.
